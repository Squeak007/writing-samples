## SECTION 1 — "The Problem"

### Freelancers lose money three ways. Snaptime fixes all three.

*1. The hours you forget to log.*
Every freelancer has had the Thursday-afternoon horror of trying to remember what they worked on Monday. Snaptime starts a timer from your calendar, your project folders, or a keyboard shortcut — so logging is passive, not active. If you touched the project, we tracked the time.

*2. The invoices you forget to send.*
On the first of the month, Snaptime drafts your invoices automatically from your tracked hours. You review, approve, send. The whole month's billing takes seven minutes.

*3. The payments you forget to chase.*
Clients forget to pay. We don't. Snaptime automatically sends polite reminders at 7, 14, and 30 days overdue, in your voice — not a robotic "This is an automated reminder" that makes you look small. Your invoices get paid 40% faster on average.

## SECTION 2 — "How it works"

### Three steps, zero setup headaches.

*Step 1: Install the timer.*
Download the Mac or Windows app (iOS and Android coming Q1 2027). Open your projects. The timer lives in your menu bar. Start it with Cmd+Shift+T or let Snaptime start it automatically when you open a project folder.

*Step 2: Connect your clients.*
Add each client once. Attach their hourly rate, your preferred invoice template, and their email. Snaptime remembers everything after that.

*Step 3: Approve and send.*
On the first of the month, Snaptime drafts invoices for every client with hours tracked that month. Review, adjust, approve, send. Payments route to your bank through Stripe. (Already have a Stripe account? Connect in 30 seconds. Don't have one? We'll walk you through it.)

## SECTION 5 — "FAQ"

*Does Snaptime work offline?*
Yes. The timer runs locally. Your hours sync when you reconnect.

*Which invoicing systems do you integrate with?*
Snaptime generates its own invoices and sends via Stripe for payment. If you prefer QuickBooks, Xero, or FreshBooks, we export in formats they accept.

*Can I use my own invoice template?*
Yes. Upload a template once, Snaptime populates it from your tracked hours.

*What if a client disputes my hours?*
Every tracked hour includes a time-stamped activity log. If a client questions a line item, you have receipts.

*Do you take a cut of my invoices?*
No. Snaptime is a flat monthly subscription. Payment processing fees are Stripe's standard rates (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction), which go to Stripe, not to us.

*Can I try it before buying?*
Yes. 14 days free. No credit card required to start.
